Don't Let Anyone Take Away Your Big Box of Cables

Jim Nielsen · captured Sep 11, 2026 · complete · verified ✓

Speaking of being on the internet and finding things that make you go, "Hey! It's not just me!" — I have a big box of cables. Adapters, dongles, wall-warts. A museum of connectivity.

Every few years someone writes an essay about throwing it all away. Minimalism! And every few years, the box saves me: a friend's old hard drive, a hotel TV that only takes coax, a keyboard from 2004 that still works perfectly.

The cables aren't clutter. They're optionality. They're the physical form of "I might need this later" — and later always comes.

Summary

A short essay defending the habit of keeping spare cables: they convert future inconvenience into a solved problem.
